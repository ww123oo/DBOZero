解壓方式（在 data/舊譯表/deltas_merged/ 目錄）：

  python -c "import base64,gzip; from pathlib import Path
for n in ['term','ui']:
  p=Path(n+'.tsv.gz.b64')
  if p.exists():
    Path(n+'.tsv').write_bytes(gzip.decompress(base64.b64decode(p.read_text().strip())))
    print('wrote', n+'.tsv')"
